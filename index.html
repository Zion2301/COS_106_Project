Brief biography

A student who ships

I'm a Computer Science student at Miva Open University and the founder and CEO of Provara, a booking marketplace that connects people in Port Harcourt with barbers, hairstylists and nail technicians.

I built and launched the app on both iOS and Android while studying, and I enjoy turning real local problems into working products. I'm driven by building things that people actually use.

This website is my COS 106 (Introduction to Web Technologies) term project. It brings together my profile, my projects and a small academic planner I use to keep track of coursework.
