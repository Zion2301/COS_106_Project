    How to use the planner

    1. Type a task, and optionally add a course code and due date.
    2. Press Add Task or hit Enter.
    3. Tick the checkbox to mark a task as completed.
    4. Press 🗑 to delete a task.
    5. Use the filters to show All, Active or Completed tasks.

    Study tips

    • Break big assignments into small tasks.
    • Add due dates so overdue work is highlighted in red.
    • Review your list at the start of every study session.
